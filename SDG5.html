Gender Equality

Ensuring men and women are treated equally in all fields. This is one of the most common issues of the Sustainable Development Goals.

Gender Equality is an important issue

Giving equal opportunities and equal pay to both men and women is an important part of uniting the world, as gender inequality is an issue that affects half of the worlds population.

All people deserve the same opportunities, no matter who they were born as.
